The pipeline

From raw audio to answered questions.

01

Capture

Wavelength pulls audio from a YouTube link or your upload and splits it into clean, processable chunks.

02

Transcribe

Every chunk is transcribed in your chosen language, then stitched back into one continuous script.

03

Understand

A title, summary, action items, key decisions and open questions are pulled straight out of the transcript.

04

Converse

The transcript is indexed for retrieval, so you can ask follow‑up questions and get grounded answers.
